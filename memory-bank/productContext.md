# Product Context

## Problem Statement
Gyms need a way to display promotional content, announcements, and branding on screens throughout their facilities. Current solutions are expensive, complex, or require constant manual updates.

## Solution
A kiosk application that automatically syncs content from Google Drive folders, allowing gym operators to:
- Upload media to organized folders
- Have content automatically appear on displays
- Support location-specific content
- Work offline once synced

## Target Users
- Gym operators and managers
- Marketing teams
- Facility managers

## Business Value
- Reduced manual content management
- Centralized media distribution
- Location-specific messaging
- Cost-effective display solution
- Easy content updates via Google Drive
